Worth acting on (ranked by fit to JARVIS)

1. 2 MB large-page mapping of the model region — "Reserved THP" (Jun 29). This is the most actionable item, and it's yours to do, not a llama.cpp feature to port. Your goal-#1 conclusion is that decode is memory-bandwidth-bound — you scan the full ~2.9 GB Gemma weight set every token. Backing that region with 2 MB pages instead of 4 KB cuts TLB misses on exactly that scan, and seL4 already lets you choose mapping granularity (your device-init already does a 2 MB LargePage skip). This could yield a real, measurable tok/s bump on the bottleneck with no external depeate for the seL4 build.

2. Per-head adaptive KV-cache quant — llama.cpp #21385 (Jun 28). Production A/B shows q4_0 KV cache is BLEU-1.000 lossless on
hybrid models, and Gemma 4 runs only ~10/60 layersing/linear), so the quant noise gets absorbed. Thisis squarely on your deployed Gemma 4 E2B and is the reference to read before revisiting the TQ/RQ KV-compression you deferred to Phase 4. Bonus: it intersects a real bug my codebase sweep just found — llama_load.c:663 over-allocates the KV cache ~57% for shared-KV Gemma. KV is a live, specific lead.

3. Gemma 4 QAT GGUFs (noted Jun 26 / Jun 30). Google's quantization-aware checkpoints → Unsloth QAT GGUFs, E2B ~3 GB UD-Q4_K_XL — same arch, same size class as what you run, better quality. The easiest quality upgrade for the deployed model whenever you want it; should load on your existing engine unchanged.

4. strncpy() removal lesson — Linux 7.2 (Jun 23). atches your March/April audit discipline. Worth a10-minute grep of the hand-rolled parsers (FAT32 / NVMe / BPE tokenizer / shmem IPC / the [PANEL]/log string paths) for strncpy/strncat and a move to bounded, always-terminated copies. I can run that grep now if you want.                              
5. EAGLE3 speculative decoding in llama.cpp (Jun 17). The biggest theoretical tok/s lever and the closest thing to "close the gap to llama.cpp" — but the heaviest: needs a draft model + extra KV memory. A Phase-4-perf-revisit experiment, not a quick win.
