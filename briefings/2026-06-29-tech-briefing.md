# JARVIS Daily Tech Briefing — Monday, June 29, 2026

**Lead — A new Linux MM proposal, "Reserved THP," merges HugeTLB's guarantees with THP's transparency (RFC, Jun 27).** Bytedance's Qi Zheng posted an RFC for *Reserved THP*: reserve huge pages up front (HugeTLB-style, so you're guaranteed to get them) but map them transparently (THP-style, no special allocator API or `MAP_HUGETLB` dance). *Why it matters:* your Phase-4 goal-#1 conclusion is that inference is **memory-bandwidth-bound (~2.9 GB/token)** — you scan the entire ~2.9 GB Gemma weight set every token. Backing that region with **2 MB large pages instead of 4 KB** cuts TLB misses on exactly that scan, and seL4 already lets you pick the mapping granularity (you map the GOP FB and devices explicitly today, and the device-init notes already use a 2 MB LargePage skip). This RFC is mainline working through the guaranteed-huge-page tradeoff — a direct reference for whether JARVIS should map the model region with large pages on the seL4 side. Caveat: it's a Linux RFC, not landed, and JARVIS isn't Linux — the lever is the concept, not the code. [Phoronix](https://www.phoronix.com/news/Reserved-THP-Linux)
