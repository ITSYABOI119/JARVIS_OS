**Lead — NetBSD 11.0 shipped (formal release date Jul 30; announced/picked up in the Aug 1–2 window): the 19th major release, and the headline for a bare-metal x86 dev is a new MICROVM x86/amd64 kernel variant that boots a full VM in roughly 10 ms on 2020-era hardware.** Also in it: the project's first stable 64-bit RISC-V port (StarFive JH71xx — VisionFive 2 / STAR64), initial Snapdragon X1 Elite support, POSIX.1-2024 + C23 coverage, and expanded Linux-syscall (`compat_linux`) compatibility.
