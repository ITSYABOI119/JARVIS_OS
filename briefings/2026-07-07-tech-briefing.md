## Local LLM Inference

- **llama.cpp PR #25346 — `ggml-cpu: detect AVX-VNNI in MSVC native builds` (opened Jul 6).** Fixes AVX-VNNI feature detection for native Visual Studio builds of the ggml CPU backend. *Why it matters — as a marker, not a change you'll use:* AVX-VNNI (`vpdpbusd`) is the hardware int8×int8→int32 dot-product-accumulate instruction — the primitive a "true int8 qdot" would use, versus your `qdot`'s dequant-*inside*-the-dot over AVX2-FMA floats. Doubly N/A to JARVIS: it's **MSVC-build-only** (you build with gcc 13.3), and your deploy box's **Zen+ 2700X predates AVX-VNNI entirely** (Intel Alder Lake+ / AMD Zen 4–5 only). So it's a reminder of the int8-dot hardware path you're *not* on — and why the float-FMA qdot is the right call for that silicon — not a portable optimization.
