*Not 1–2-day news, but on-point and never briefed — worth a look:*

- **LUT-based low-bit SIMD dot products — two references straight in your AVX2 `qdot` lane.** (1) **llama.cpp PR #23961** adds an **AVX2 nvfp4 (NVIDIA FP4) dot-product kernel via a UE4M3 lookup table**: AVX2 has no native FP4, so it precomputes the 4-bit-float→value table and runs the dot in SIMD over the LUT — the same "dequant *inside* the dot kernel" move your `qdot` makes for Q4_K/Q6_K, applied to a 4-bit *float*. (2) **Vec-LUT (arXiv:2512.06443)** generalizes it: vectorized table lookup for *parallel ultra-low-bit* CPU inference on edge devices (the T-MAC / LUT-GEMM lineage). *Why it matters:* if JARVIS ever pushes below Q4 on the CPU path, table-lookup dot products are the established way to make sub-8-bit *fast* on a vector ISA with no native support — a concrete pattern for extending your AVX2 kernels instead of falling back to scalar. Both predate the window (PR merged earlier in 2026; paper Dec 2025). [llama.cpp #23961](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/pull/23961) · [Vec-LUT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.06443)
